# Hardspace Shipbreaker

## Where is it?

Hardspace: Shipbreaker is a zero-G salvage sim by Blackbird Interactive. Career mode has you dismantle decommissioned ships to pay off LYNX debt while unlocking tools, ranks, and ship classes.

## What does randomization do?

This world randomizes Career progression for multiworld play:

- **Locations:** bay deposits, salvage parts, family/variant salvage tiers (1–5), grade clears, Hab shop purchases (when shop-sanity is on), cert ranks, data drives, and related milestones.
- **Items:** progressive Hab equipment (tether, grapple, demo, scanners, suit, cutter, charged push), Progressive Certification Rank, credit/LT filler, optional Clone Fee Tax trap.
- **Credit Pack Value** (YAML): how much debt fillers pay — `low` / `normal` (default 1M / 3M / 8M) / `high`.
- **Ship families:** Atlas / Javelin / Gecko appear through **vanilla certification** as Progressive Certification Rank raises the MP ceiling. There are no placed AP “Unlock Atlas/Javelin/Gecko” items.
- **Goals:** pay off LYNX debt (default), reach **Atlas Scout Salvage Tier 5**, or reach Certification Rank 20.

Career mode is the supported path. Free Play is deferred.

## Unique local commands

None. The BepInEx client uses in-game hotkeys for connect / debug (see setup guide).
